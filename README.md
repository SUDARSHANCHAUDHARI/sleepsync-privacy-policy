# Privacy Policy — SleepSync

**Last updated:** 2026-03-21
**Effective date:** 2026-03-21

SleepSync is developed and operated by **SudarshanTechLabs**, Bangkok, Thailand.

This Privacy Policy explains how SleepSync handles your information when you use our application.

---

## 1. Data Collection

SleepSync collects only the data you explicitly provide and the data generated by your use of the app.

**Data stored locally on your device:**
- Sleep session records (start time, end time, duration)
- Sleep quality ratings and mood logs
- Smart alarm settings and history
- Sleep sound preferences
- User profile settings (name, sleep goals, target schedule)
- Notification preferences

**Data we do NOT collect:**
- We do not collect your name, email address, or any personally identifiable information unless you voluntarily enter it in the app profile
- We do not collect device identifiers, advertising IDs, or location data
- We do not use analytics SDKs, crash reporting services, or advertising networks

---

## 2. How We Use Your Data

All data collected by SleepSync is used solely to provide the app's features:

- **Sleep tracking:** storing and displaying your sleep history
- **Smart alarm:** analysing your sleep session to determine the optimal wake time
- **AI insights:** sending anonymised sleep statistics to Google Gemini AI to generate personalised sleep recommendations (see Section 3)
- **Sleep sounds:** remembering your preferred sounds and timer settings
- **Insights & charts:** calculating sleep statistics and trends

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

### Google Gemini AI
SleepSync uses the Google Gemini AI API to generate personalised sleep insights. When you request AI-powered recommendations, a summary of your recent sleep data (duration, quality scores, sleep times — with no personally identifiable information) is sent to Google's Gemini API.

This data is subject to Google's Privacy Policy:
[https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### No Other Third Parties
SleepSync does not integrate with any advertising networks, analytics platforms, social media SDKs, or other third-party data processors.

---

## 4. Data Storage & Security

- All sleep data is stored **locally on your device** using Android's secure local storage (Room database with SQLite).
- No data is stored on SudarshanTechLabs servers.
- No data is transmitted off your device except as described in Section 3 (Gemini AI).
- We use Android's `security-crypto` library for encrypted local storage where applicable.
- You can delete all app data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing app data in your device settings.

---

## 5. Permissions

SleepSync requests the following Android permissions:

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `INTERNET` | Required for AI insights via Gemini API |
| `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` | Check connectivity before API calls |
| `WAKE_LOCK` | Keep CPU active during sleep tracking |
| `FOREGROUND_SERVICE` | Run sleep tracking in the background |
| `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK` | Play sleep sounds while screen is off |
| `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC` | Run sleep tracking service |
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | Smart alarm and sleep tracking notifications |
| `VIBRATE` | Alarm vibration |
| `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` | Schedule precise smart alarm |
| `USE_EXACT_ALARM` | Ensure alarm fires at correct time |
| `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` | Restore alarms after device reboot |
| `ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION` | Detect sleep/wake transitions (optional) |

No permission is used for advertising, tracking, or data collection beyond the stated purpose.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

SleepSync is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

---

## 7. Data Retention

- Sleep data is stored on your device for as long as you use the app
- You can delete individual records from within the app
- Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data
